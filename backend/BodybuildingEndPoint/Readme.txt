Collated and curated the exercises from my personal experience and some from the internet. Used Google Sheets to collate exercises and 
CSV2JSON convertor to develop the endpoint. Hosting the endpoint on jsonbin.io. 

This endpoint can be used by developers who are building a front end application for Bodybuilders either for  Mobile(iOS and Android) or 
Web. Have built the endpoint keeping in mind the compatibility of all the ecosystems. 

Feel free to use this endpoint for your application, reach out to me incase of error in the endpoint or data. Do not forget to leave
your contact details for communication related to the endpoint. Look forward to seeing your Bodybuilding applications. 

Endpoint is mentioned in a separate file named "BodybuildingEndpoint"
